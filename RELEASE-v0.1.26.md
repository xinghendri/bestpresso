# Bestpresso v0.1.26

## New
- Unified Settings brings Bestpresso preferences and supported Decaid controls into one place: drink preparation, cleaning, connected devices, power, display, and more.
- Choose Celsius or Fahrenheit, and follow your device clock or select 12-hour or 24-hour time.
- Customize the early reservoir warning, completion sound, and chart thickness: Thin (1px), Medium (2px), or Thick (3px) across all charts.
- Hot water automatically uses weight stopping with a connected scale and shares the universal Yield look-ahead setting.

## Improved
- Refreshed card surfaces, subtle outlines, darker homescreen panels, and a stronger animated selected-profile border.
- More compact, better-aligned metric controls and tablet settings layouts.
- Settings show known homescreen values while loading; Bestpresso preference changes now use Save and Cancel.
- Completion audio now uses MP3 for broader browser compatibility.
- Improved final-yield stability after a shot completes.

## Notes
- Available machine controls depend on your Decaid version and connection.
- The machine's own needs-water state always takes priority.
